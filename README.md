# Smart Manufacturing Research

## About This Repository
This repository documents my learning and research on smart manufacturing, Industry 4.0, digital twins, and industrial energy efficiency.

## Research Objectives
- Understand emerging manufacturing technologies.
- Study how digital twins support industrial systems.
- Explore energy efficiency and waste-heat recovery.
- Identify practical engineering problems and potential solutions.

## Research Questions
1. How can Industry 4.0 improve manufacturing productivity?
2. How can digital twins help monitor industrial equipment?
3. How can manufacturing energy consumption be reduced?
4. What technical and economic challenges limit adoption?

## Research Method
I will review technical papers, document key findings, compare existing approaches, and identify opportunities for further investigation.
