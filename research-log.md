# Research Log

## Research Entry 001

### Paper Details
- Title: Energy Digital Twins in Smart Manufacturing Systems: A Case Study
- Authors: Anna Billey and Thorsten Wuest
- Year: 2024
- Journal: Robotics and Computer-Integrated Manufacturing
- Volume: 88
- Article number: 102729
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rcim.2024.102729

### Research Problem
How can a digital twin optimize energy consumption in a real manufacturing process?

### Methodology
The researchers developed a digital counterpart of a physical heating tunnel using Python. OPC UA enabled bidirectional communication between the physical process and the digital system. An optimization model calculated a suitable turn-off temperature and sent feedback to the physical process.

### Key Findings
- The system maintained the heating tunnel's maximum temperature range.
- Energy consumption and cost were reduced compared with the original process in the reported trials.
- The publisher reports energy savings of up to 40% in this case study.

### Engineering Significance
This work demonstrates how mechanical processes can be connected with software models and feedback control to improve energy efficiency.

### Limitations and Open Questions
- How well would this approach work for other manufacturing processes?
- What are the implementation and maintenance costs?
- How would results change under different operating conditions?
- Further evidence is needed before generalizing the reported energy savings.

### My Research Questions
1. Can a similar approach optimize energy use in an industrial oven?
2. Which sensors and operating parameters would be required?
3. How could energy savings be measured against a baseline?
